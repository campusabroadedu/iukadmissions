About International University of Kyrgyzstan (IUK / ISM)

The International University of Kyrgyzstan (IUK) was created by decree of the President of the Kyrgyz Republic in 1993 as a direct state-level international partnership. It operates with autonomous status as an educational, scientific, and production complex.

The International School of Medicine (ISM) operates within the IUK framework, training foreign students from over 15 nations in General Medicine (MD). Teaching adheres to modern European medical curricula delivered entirely in English with clinical clerkships across Bishkek’s top multidisciplinary hospitals.

Campus Locations: Central Academic Campus is located at 17a/1 Lev Tolstoy Street, and the specialized Eastern Medical Campus is located at 6, 7th April Street, Bishkek.
